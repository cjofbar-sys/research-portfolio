# Research lines: scientific interlocutions

The research programme contains connected questions that can be examined through different disciplinary and methodological collaborations. The following map identifies **questions for investigation**, not evidence that every field or technique has been mastered.

| Interlocution | Shared scientific problem | Necessary distinction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cultural evolution / IAST | Social learning, norms, cooperation and cultural variability | Historical and semiotic processes and population-level transmission require explicit bridges between analytical scales. |
| AI metacognition / Waterloo | Confidence, calibration, resolution, uncertainty and evaluation | Observable model confidence must not be assumed equivalent to human subjective metacognition. |
| Human–AI judgment / Waterloo | Reliance on AI, expert judgment, decision quality | Experimental designs should separate model performance, human uptake and joint-system outcomes. |
| Cultural narratives / Waterloo | Meaning of narratives and cooperative action | Changes in understanding of a task should be distinguishable from changes in cooperative behavior. |
| Wise judgment / Waterloo | Situated strategy use, measurement and cultural ecologies | Across-context comparisons demand examination of response processes, validity and measurement comparability. |
| Engineering education / Cornell | Educational assessment, reflection, identity and AI mediation | Interpretation of instrument scores depends on validity evidence and conditions of use. |

These connections will be refined with original source literature, documented experience, and the specific institutional research questions. Application-specific arguments and unpublished proposal ideas are maintained separately from this public overview.
