**Research relationship.** This article discusses socioemotional development during professional internships in higher education, with attention to metacognition, emotional regulation, and the teaching supervisor as a mediator. Its relationship to the broader research programme requires historically and conceptually precise interpretation; it is not presented as evidence of an experimental or longitudinal study.
