# Research Portfolio

**Cristian Andrés Jofré Barrera** · [Personal website](https://cristianjofrebarrera.com/) · [ORCID](https://orcid.org/0000-0003-1213-2900)

This portfolio documents an evolving research programme concerned with cultural-semiotic psychology, human development, imagination, agency, life projects, situated judgment, and human–AI mediation.

The central questions concern how possibilities for action and becoming are constituted through social relationships, cultural resources, institutional arrangements and mediated environments. The programme connects conceptual development, methodological examination and carefully described research systems.

## Methodological orientation

Research design begins from the phenomenon and the scientific question. Statistical models, qualitative analysis, simulations, instruments, and computational systems acquire significance through explicit decisions about what they can establish and what remains undetermined. The [first laboratory demonstration](05-reproducible-examples/longitudinal-multilevel/README.md) makes these questions examinable through entirely synthetic student trajectories.

## Documentation status and safeguards

This is an initial public structure. Programme summaries and project descriptions are working formulations pending validation against original manuscripts and records. Examples labelled **synthetic** do not constitute empirical findings. Scholarly publication metadata, exact professional roles and implementation claims will be expanded following documentary review. Private repositories, research participants' information, proprietary instructions and unpublished manuscripts are not reproduced here.

The website remains a separate implementation managed in Lovable. Its Research section can refer readers to approved materials in this repository.
