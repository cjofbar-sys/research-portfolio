# Academic experience and contributions

Research, teaching, psychometric work, editorial responsibilities, institutional participation, software development, research stays and service will be documented with:

- Dates and institution, verified against original records.
- Role, responsibilities and demonstrable contribution.
- Evidence and its permitted public form.
- Status of outcomes, especially for ongoing projects.

This folder deliberately does not publish personal records, employment documents, private contacts, grant applications or unverified numerical impact claims.
