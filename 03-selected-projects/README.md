# Selected research systems

This section documents selected systems as **research and software initiatives**. The descriptions are intentionally high-level and do not certify public readiness, participant recruitment, peer-reviewed validation, or deployment of every proposed feature.

## LifeProject Research App

A longitudinal research application exploring participant-facing AI interlocution and research infrastructure for studying life projects and possible futures. The design differentiates participant interaction from research oversight and downstream analysis. Implementation state, participant protection, and empirical validation must be verified before making more specific public claims.

## Research Studio

A scientific writing and research-workflow initiative that organizes sources, conceptual development, draft histories, bibliographic provenance, and editorial learning. Some documented components are architectural specifications rather than complete production integrations.

## Observatorio SAP–IA

An applied research initiative focused on evidence quality and analysis of AI in educational systems. Conceptual design and initial technical modules should be distinguished from validated or institutionally adopted outcomes.

## Publication standard for project pages

Each project profile must identify: (1) scientific problem; (2) conceptual relationship to the wider programme; (3) the researcher's documented role; (4) implementation status; (5) available evidence; (6) limitations; and (7) links approved for public distribution. Private repositories, proprietary instructions, sensitive architectures and participant material are excluded.

Further project descriptions remain pending source verification and disclosure review.
